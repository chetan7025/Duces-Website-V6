BUSINESS ACCOUNT READINESS

Build a complete
application profile.

Well-organised and consistent information can make it easier for a bank to understand your business. The exact requirements differ by bank, activity, ownership and risk assessment.

Company & ownership documents

  • Valid trade licence and commercial registration
  • Memorandum and Articles of Association, as applicable
  • Certificate of Incorporation and share certificate, where applicable
  • Passport, UAE visa and Emirates ID of shareholders and authorised signatories
  • UBO details, ownership chart and board resolution, if requested
  • Office lease, Ejari or relevant facility agreement

Business activity & financial evidence

  • Clear company profile and explanation of business activities
  • Expected monthly transaction volumes, currencies and countries
  • Customer and supplier details, contracts, invoices or purchase orders
  • Source-of-funds and source-of-wealth supporting documents
  • Existing company bank statements and financial statements, where available
  • Website, business plan or operational evidence, where relevant

This is a preparation checklist, not a universal bank document list. A bank may request additional documents, certified copies, explanations or an in-person meeting. Never alter, omit or misrepresent information in a banking application.

BANKING & FINANCE QUESTIONS

Know what to expect
before you apply.

Answers to common questions about UAE business banking and finance applications.

Does a UAE trade licence guarantee a business bank account?

No. A trade licence is only one part of the application. Each bank independently reviews the business activity, shareholders, source of funds, expected transactions, customers, suppliers and risk profile. Account opening is never guaranteed.

Can a newly formed company apply for a business bank account?

Some banks and digital providers consider newly formed businesses, while others may expect operating history, contracts, invoices or other evidence. The options depend on the company's activity, ownership, documentation and the institution's current policy.

What is the difference between a personal and business bank account?

A business account is designed for company transactions and is opened in the legal entity's name. Company income and expenses should be managed through appropriate business accounts in line with accounting, tax and banking requirements.

How long does business account opening take?

There is no universal timeline. Processing depends on the bank's review, completeness of documents, business complexity, due diligence and any additional information requests. The bank confirms its own expected timeline.

Can a company apply for business finance without an existing bank account?

Requirements vary by lender and product. Some providers may require an account with them, while others may review statements from an existing bank. The lender's current eligibility rules should be checked before preparing an application.

What financial information is usually required for business finance?

Depending on the product, lenders may review company bank statements, turnover, audited or management accounts, trade invoices, existing liabilities, ownership documents and the purpose of funding. Additional evidence may be requested.

Can DUCES guarantee a bank account or finance approval?

No. DUCES is an independent consultancy, not a bank or lender. We can help organise information and coordinate an enquiry, but account opening, credit decisions, rates, terms and approvals are solely at the discretion of the relevant financial institution.
