# DUCES Website V6

This repository contains the DUCES Consultancy Services website.

## V6 review improvements
- Reinforced the approved brand palette: Royal Blue `#123B70`, Corporate Blue `#1976B9`, Emerald Green `#008F6B`, Champagne Gold `#D9AD55`, white and soft neutrals.
- Corrected the final inner-hero CSS override so service pages do not reuse the Home hero photograph. Banking & Finance retains its dedicated banking hero image.

- Added a general privacy notice and linked it from the site footer.
- Completed the sitemap and corrected robots.txt formatting.
- Added image loading hints, visible keyboard focus styles, reduced-motion handling and responsive navigation refinements.

## Enquiry form
The contact form validates required fields and prepares an email in the visitor's email application. The visitor must press Send; the website does not transmit the enquiry automatically. Automatic submissions are intentionally not connected until a form provider is selected and configured, the destination email is verified, and the provider's data-processing terms are reviewed. Do not publish a placeholder endpoint.

## Publishing
The GitHub Pages workflow deploys from `main`. Changes on the review branch are not live until they are reviewed and merged into `main`.

## Launch checks
Source-level checks do not replace a full browser/device review. Before launch, test all pages on desktop and mobile, test every contact link and the enquiry email hand-off, and review the privacy notice against the actual contracting entity and data-handling practices.
