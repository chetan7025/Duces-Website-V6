ACCOUNTING & TAX QUESTIONS

Common questions,
clear answers.

A few important points to understand before arranging accounting or tax support.

Does every UAE company need bookkeeping?

Businesses should maintain appropriate accounting records that explain their transactions and financial position. The records needed and any additional audit or reporting requirements depend on the entity, activity and applicable law.

When should a business register for VAT?

VAT registration depends on taxable supplies and imports and the applicable mandatory or voluntary registration criteria. The threshold test and exceptions should be checked against current Federal Tax Authority guidance and the business’s actual figures.

Do Free Zone companies have to consider Corporate Tax?

Yes. A Free Zone company should assess its Corporate Tax registration and filing obligations. A 0% rate may apply to qualifying income of an entity that meets the conditions for Qualifying Free Zone Person status; it is not an automatic exemption for every Free Zone business.

Can DUCES help with VAT and Corporate Tax filing?

DUCES can help organise the information and coordinate the requirement with relevant tax professionals. Any tax advice, representation or filing is subject to the credentials and authorisations of the professional engaged for the work.

What if my company has not maintained its accounts regularly?

The first step is to assess the records available, missing periods, bank statements and outstanding tax filings. A qualified accounting professional can then scope any catch-up bookkeeping, reconciliations and corrective filings required.

How often should bookkeeping be updated?

Monthly bookkeeping is a practical approach for many operating businesses because it helps keep transactions, reconciliations and management information current. The appropriate frequency depends on transaction volume and business needs.

Does DUCES provide statutory audit opinions?

DUCES does not represent itself as a statutory audit firm. Where an audit is required, we can help coordinate with an appropriately licensed independent audit firm.
