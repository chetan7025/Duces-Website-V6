BUSINESS SETUP QUESTIONS

Common questions,
clear answers.

Helpful guidance before you choose a UAE company structure. The final requirements depend on your activity, authority and circumstances.

Should I choose a Mainland or Free Zone company?

Mainland may be suitable when you need to operate directly in the UAE domestic market or require premises and approvals associated with a mainland licence. A Free Zone may suit businesses whose activities, customers and operating model fit a particular zone. Compare your actual activity, customer locations, visa needs, premises, banking and total annual costs before deciding.

Can a foreign investor own 100% of a UAE company?

Many UAE business activities allow full foreign ownership, including many mainland and Free Zone structures. Certain strategic, regulated or activity-specific cases may have additional conditions or approvals. The proposed activity and legal form must be checked with the relevant authority.

Can I establish a UAE company if I live outside the UAE?

Non-residents can establish many UAE company structures. However, signing, document verification, immigration, office and banking procedures differ by authority and bank. Some steps may require your presence in the UAE.

Can a Free Zone company do business with UAE Mainland customers?

This depends on the licensed activity, the type of goods or services, the contractual arrangement and applicable mainland, customs and regulatory rules. A mainland distributor, branch, permit or other compliant arrangement may be needed in some cases. Confirm the proposed sales model before trading.

How many business activities can I include on one licence?

Some authorities allow multiple compatible activities on one licence. Activities from different categories or those requiring separate regulatory approvals may need additional permissions or a separate licence. The authority must confirm the permitted combination.

What documents are usually needed to form a company?

Common documents include shareholder passport copies, UAE visa and Emirates ID where applicable, contact and residential details, proposed company names, activity details and shareholding information. Corporate shareholders or regulated activities may require additional company documents, resolutions, attestations or approvals.

How long does company formation take?

Timelines vary by jurisdiction, activity, document readiness, name approval and any external clearances. A straightforward application may move faster than one involving regulated activities, corporate shareholders or document legalisation. We confirm an estimated timeline after reviewing the case; authority processing times are not guaranteed.

Does a Free Zone company automatically qualify for 0% Corporate Tax?

No. A Free Zone company is not automatically tax-free. A 0% Corporate Tax rate may apply only to qualifying income of a Qualifying Free Zone Person that meets the applicable legal conditions. Registration, returns, accounting records and other compliance obligations may still apply. Obtain advice based on the company's actual activities and income.

Will a company licence guarantee a UAE business bank account?

No. A trade licence does not guarantee bank account approval. Each bank independently reviews the shareholders, business activity, source of funds, expected transactions, customers, suppliers and supporting documents. DUCES can help organise the application and business profile, but the bank makes the final decision.

Can my company provide me with a UAE residence visa?

Some eligible mainland and Free Zone companies can apply for investor, partner or employee visas, subject to the authority's rules, visa allocation, establishment requirements and immigration approval. The number of visas and total cost depend on the selected package and circumstances. Visa issuance is not guaranteed.

What should I plan for after the trade licence is issued?

Depending on the company, next steps may include establishment and immigration registration, visas, corporate bank account application, Corporate Tax registration and filing, VAT assessment, bookkeeping, audit or other regulatory filings, and timely licence renewal. The post-licence checklist should be confirmed for your activity and jurisdiction.

What does the advertised setup package price include?

Package inclusions differ by authority and offer. Before paying, request a written breakdown covering the licence, registration, permitted activities, office or flexi-desk, visa allocation, establishment card, medical and Emirates ID, government charges, deposits, renewals and any professional fees. Do not assume an item is included unless it is listed in writing.
