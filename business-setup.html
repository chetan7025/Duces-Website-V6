START WITH THE RIGHT QUESTIONS

A licence is only
one part of the decision.

A suitable UAE structure should reflect how the business will actually operate. The lowest advertised package may not include the office arrangement, visa allocation, approvals or ongoing costs your business needs.

DUCES helps you organise the decision, understand the likely requirements and coordinate with the relevant licensing authority and independent professional providers where needed.

THREE COMMON ROUTES

Understand your
formation options.

Each route has its own permitted activities, rules, costs and limitations. The correct choice depends on the facts of your proposed business.

01 / UAE MARKET
M

Mainland Company

A mainland licence may suit businesses that need to operate directly in the UAE domestic market, work with local customers or pursue activities that require a mainland structure.

Considerations

  • Exact activity and any external approvals
  • Legal form and ownership requirements
  • Office or premises requirements
  • Visa quota and immigration arrangements
Discuss Mainland setup →
02 / FREE ZONE ECOSYSTEM
F

Free Zone Company

A Free Zone structure may suit businesses looking for a specific authority ecosystem, defined licence packages or an operating model aligned with that zone's rules.

Considerations

  • Permitted activities and business model
  • Visa eligibility and allocation
  • Flexi-desk, office or lease conditions
  • Restrictions on conducting mainland business
Discuss Free Zone setup →
03 / HOLDING STRUCTURE
O

Offshore Company

An Offshore structure may be considered for eligible international holding or asset-structuring purposes. It is not a substitute for an operating licence where local business activity is required.

Considerations

  • Purpose and intended jurisdictions
  • Banking and substance expectations
  • Ownership and due diligence documents
  • Limits on local operations and visas

HOW WE ASSESS YOUR REQUIREMENT

Choose on fit,
not just on price.

Before comparing packages, we recommend reviewing the business activity and the practical requirements that follow from it.

01

Business activity

What will the company sell or provide? Does the activity need a special approval or professional qualification?

02

Customers and geography

Will you serve UAE mainland customers, Free Zone businesses, overseas clients or a mix?

03

People and premises

How many visas are needed? Is a desk, shared facility, warehouse, shop or dedicated office required?

04

Banking and compliance

Can you explain the source of funds, expected transactions, customer profile and supporting business records?

05

Total cost of operation

Consider renewals, visas, establishment cards, office costs, approvals, tax and accounting—not only the first licence fee.

DOCUMENT READINESS

Prepare the basics
before you apply.

The exact list depends on the authority, legal form, activity and shareholder profile. Additional documents may be requested.

Commonly requested information

  • Passport copy for each shareholder
  • UAE visa and Emirates ID, if applicable
  • Proposed company name and business activities
  • Contact details and residential address
  • Shareholding split and management details
  • Business plan or activity-specific supporting documents, where required

Do not submit original documents or pay fees until the issuing authority, scope and payment recipient have been verified.
