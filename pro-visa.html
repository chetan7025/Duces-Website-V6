PRO & VISA QUESTIONS

Know what to
expect next.

General answers to common UAE administrative questions. Confirm current requirements for your specific application before proceeding.

Does DUCES issue visas or Emirates IDs?

No. DUCES provides guidance and coordination support. Visas, Emirates IDs, work permits and related approvals are issued or processed by the relevant UAE government authorities and authorised channels.

Can you guarantee visa approval?

No. Every application is subject to eligibility checks, document review and the decision of the relevant authority. We do not guarantee approval, issuance time or a particular outcome.

What is an establishment card?

An establishment or immigration card is part of the company-level registration used for relevant immigration transactions. The applicable process, validity and renewal requirements depend on the company and issuing authority.

Is a medical fitness test required for a UAE residence visa?

Medical fitness screening is generally part of the residence visa process for applicants in applicable age and visa categories. The exact requirements and exemptions, if any, must be confirmed for the applicant and current rules.

How long does a visa or Emirates ID application take?

Processing time varies by application type, authority workload, appointment availability, document quality and any additional checks. We can help track the process, but authority timelines are not guaranteed.

Can you help with visa renewal or cancellation?

We can help identify the likely steps and coordinate the relevant renewal or cancellation process. The sponsor, applicant status, expiry date, outstanding fines or obligations and authority rules may affect the procedure.

Can I apply for a family visa through my UAE residence?

Eligible UAE residents may sponsor qualifying family members if they meet the current sponsor, income, accommodation and documentation requirements. Eligibility and the required evidence should be checked before applying.

Do you provide document attestation and translation?

DUCES can help identify the required document route and coordinate with relevant authorised translation or attestation providers where needed. The required chain depends on the document type, country of issue and receiving authority.
