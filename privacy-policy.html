How information shared with DUCES through this website and its enquiry channels is handled.

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This notice explains the intended handling of information that you choose to provide to DUCES Consultancy Services through this website. It should be read together with any separate engagement terms or privacy notices that apply to a specific service.

1. Information you choose to provide

You may choose to provide your name, mobile number, email address, selected service and details about your enquiry. If you contact DUCES by email or WhatsApp, the information you send through those services may also be received.

2. How the website enquiry form works

The enquiry form prepares a message in your device's default email application addressed to chetanpatil.2798@gmail.com. The website does not automatically transmit the form contents to a DUCES server. You decide whether to send the prepared email. If you do not have an email application configured, the form may not complete the hand-off.

5. External services and links

This website uses Google Fonts and links to WhatsApp. Those services are operated by third parties and may process technical or usage information under their own terms and privacy policies. DUCES does not control their independent data practices.

6. Sensitive information and security

Please do not send passwords, PINs, one-time passcodes, full payment-card details or other security credentials. Share identity, financial and company documents only through a secure channel agreed for the relevant engagement. Reasonable care should be taken to limit information to what is necessary for the enquiry or service.

7. Retention and your requests

Information received may be retained for as long as reasonably required to respond to an enquiry, administer an engagement, maintain business records or meet applicable legal obligations. You may contact DUCES to ask about information you have provided or request correction where appropriate.

This notice may be updated when website functionality or business practices change. The current version will be published on this page.
