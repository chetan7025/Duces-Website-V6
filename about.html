OUR ROLE

Independent guidance.
Clear responsibility.

DUCES is an independent consultancy. We are not a bank, government department, licensing authority, immigration authority or tax authority. Banks, government bodies and other regulated providers make their own decisions and may request additional information. Any approval, licence, account, finance facility, visa or tax outcome is subject to their independent review and applicable rules.

Where a service requires a licensed professional or authorised provider, the relevant work is referred to or coordinated with that provider as appropriate. We do not promise guaranteed approvals or outcomes.
